# Bearing

## Language

**Explicit save**:
A person's choice to store their current Note, Journal reflection, or Journal template, with local changes kept until that save succeeds. Untouched Journal prompts stay virtual, and leaving with unsaved changes asks for confirmation.

**Coach execution**:
The running of a Coach reply's tools with the person's turn context, attempt limits, and safe error reporting. Daily close chooses which tools and writes that reply permits.

**Owned Task**:
A Task that belongs to a person's account through its Area, including a hidden Area. Completed Tasks remain owned and available to read, but cannot be changed by open Task commands.

**Many-times habit**:
A habit tracked by the number of times it happens in a day, with a daily goal or an optional limit when Bad, instead of a streak.

**Habit goal**:
The number of times a many-times habit needs to happen for the day to be done, or an optional limit on a Bad habit.

**Habit count**:
The number of times a many-times habit happened on a particular day. It can exceed the goal.

**Weekly habit**:
A habit tracked once for a Monday–Sunday week in the person’s local dates, with streak and record counted in weeks.

**Due habit**:
A habit that is not yet done for its period, and never Bad. The Coach calls these habits due.

**Bad habit**:
A habit whose check or count records that a lapse happened, with no streak or record and no contribution to the unfinished count. A Bad many-times habit may have no goal, or a goal that acts as a limit; it is never done or due.
