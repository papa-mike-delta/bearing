# Bearing

## Language

**Explicit save**:
A person's choice to store their current Note, Journal reflection, or Journal template, with local changes kept until that save succeeds. Untouched Journal prompts stay virtual, and leaving with unsaved changes asks for confirmation.

**Coach execution**:
The running of a Coach reply's tools with the person's turn context, attempt limits, and safe error reporting. Daily close chooses which tools and writes that reply permits.

**Owned Task**:
A Task that belongs to a person's account through its Area, including a hidden Area. Completed Tasks remain owned and available to read, but cannot be changed by open Task commands.

**Many-times habit**:
A habit tracked by the number of times it happens in a day or a Monday–Sunday week, with a goal for that period or an optional limit when Bad, instead of a streak.

**Habit goal**:
The number of times a many-times habit needs to happen for its day or week to be done, or an optional limit on a Bad habit.

**Habit count**:
The number of times a many-times habit happened on a particular day or Monday–Sunday week. It can exceed the goal; daily counts go up to 99 and weekly counts up to 999.

**Weekly habit**:
A habit tracked once or many-times for a Monday–Sunday week in the person’s local dates. Once-habits that are not Bad have streak and record counted in weeks; many-times habits show the week’s count.

**Due habit**:
A habit that is not yet done for its period, and never Bad. The Coach calls these habits due.

**Bad habit**:
A habit whose check or count records that a lapse happened, with no streak or record and no contribution to the unfinished count. A Bad many-times habit may have no goal, or a goal that acts as a limit; it is never done or due.

**Light or dark**:
A person's Light, Dark, or System appearance choice, saved in that browser only and never on their account; another browser or device follows its own choice, and an unset or unknown choice follows the system. It works on top of whichever Theme is active. Not to be confused with Theme.

**Theme**:
The colour palette and radius a person chooses for their account, either one of the six built-ins or their one imported preset, saved on their account and applied on every device. Each Theme has a light and a dark version, picked by Light or dark. Not to be confused with Light or dark.
